# 40-Day Urdu Foreign Learner Course — Full Curriculum (Complete Content)

This document contains the complete day-by-day content: every vocabulary word, example sentence, exercise, and assessment question for the 40-day course. Pair this with the architecture/rationale doc for the "why" behind the structure.

**Legend:** RU = Roman Urdu, EN = English. Gendered verb forms shown as (m)/(f) where relevant — default examples use neutral/male form for simplicity, course should let user pick "I am male/female" once at start to auto-adjust verb endings app-side.

# PHASE 1: FOUNDATIONS (Days 1–10)


## Day 1 — Greetings

**New Vocabulary:**
| RU | EN | Example Sentence |
|---|---|---|
| Assalam-o-Alaikum | Hello (peace be upon you) | Assalam-o-Alaikum, kaise hain aap? |
| Walaikum Assalam | Reply to hello | Walaikum Assalam, main theek hoon. |
| Kaise hain? | How are you? (formal) | Aap kaise hain? |
| Theek hoon | I am fine | Main theek hoon, shukriya. |
| Shukriya | Thank you | Shukriya, aap bohat acche hain. |

**Grammar:** None yet — pure recognition day (listen/recognize only, no production).

**Exercises:**
- *Flashcards:* All 5 words, audio (later) + Roman Urdu + English.
- *MCQ 1:* "What does 'Shukriya' mean?" → a) Sorry b) Thank you ✔ c) Hello d) Goodbye
- *MCQ 2:* "How do you reply to 'Assalam-o-Alaikum'?" → a) Theek hoon b) Shukriya c) Walaikum Assalam ✔ d) Khuda Hafiz
- *Match the word:* Assalam-o-Alaikum↔Hello, Theek hoon↔I am fine, Shukriya↔Thank you, Kaise hain↔How are you
- *Wrap Quiz (5Q):* 3 MCQ (meaning recall) + 2 match-the-word, all from today's 5 words.

## Day 2 — Pronouns & "To Be"

**New Vocabulary:**
| RU | EN | Example Sentence |
|---|---|---|
| Main | I | Main theek hoon. |
| Tum | You (informal) | Tum kaise ho? |
| Aap | You (formal/respectful) | Aap kaise hain? |
| Woh | He/She/It | Woh acha hai. |
| Hum | We | Hum theek hain. |

**Grammar:** "hoon / ho / hain / hai" — the verb "to be" changes with the pronoun:
Main + hoon | Tum + ho | Aap/Woh/Hum + hain (Aap is plural-respect form) | Woh (singular) + hai

**Exercises:**
- *Flashcards:* 5 pronouns.
- *MCQ:* "Which word means 'I'?" → a) Tum b) Main ✔ c) Woh d) Hum
- *Match:* Main↔I, Tum↔You(informal), Aap↔You(formal), Woh↔He/She, Hum↔We
- *Sentence Build (single slot):* "___ theek hoon" with options [Main / Tum / Woh] → Answer: Main
- *Sentence Build 2:* "___ kaise hain?" with options [Aap / Main] → Answer: Aap
- *Wrap Quiz (5Q):* 2 MCQ + 1 match + 2 sentence-build (fill correct pronoun).

## Day 6 — Question Words

**New Vocabulary:**
| RU | EN | Example Sentence |

| Kaun | Who | Woh kaun hai? |
| Kahan | Where | Aap kahan hain? |
| Kab | When | Kab aayenge aap? |
| Kyun | Why | Kyun nahi? |

**Grammar:** Question words go at the start (or naturally where the unknown info sits) — "Yeh kya hai?" = "This what is?" Urdu keeps verb at the end.

**Exercises:**
- *Flashcards:* 5 question words.

- *Translation (EN→RU):* "What is this?" → Yeh kya hai?

- *Match:* Kya↔What, Kaun↔Who, Kahan↔Where, Kab↔When, Kyun↔Why
- *Wrap Quiz (5Q):* 2 MCQ + 2 translation + 1 match.

## Day 10 — MILESTONE ASSESSMENT 1 (Full Phase 1 Review)

**Quiz (15 questions, mixed):**
1–4: MCQ recall from Days 1, 2, 6, 8 vocabulary
5–6: Match sets (numbers, colors)
7–8: Fill-in-blank (pronoun + adjective placement)
9–10: Sentence build ("Yeh mera ghar hai" / "Woh kaun hai?")
11–12: Translation EN→RU ("I am fine" / "What is this?")
13: Translation RU→EN ("Woh acha dost hai")
14–15: Cumulative MCQ mixing 2 phases' worth of words

**Guided Production Task — "Introduce Yourself" (first speaking/writing milestone):**
Learner fills a template by choosing/typing from learned words:
> "Assalam-o-Alaikum. Main ___ hoon. Main theek hoon. Yeh mera dost hai. Yeh mera ghar hai. Shukriya!"
(Blank = their name; rest reuses Phase 1 vocabulary only — achievable by every learner who completed Days 1–9.)

**Pass threshold:** 10/15 + completed production task to unlock Phase 2.

## Day 40 — FINAL MILESTONE ASSESSMENT (Capstone)

**Capstone Production Task — Open-Ended Guided Conversation Simulation:**
Learner completes a full simulated conversation by filling in their own responses at each prompt (system gives the "other speaker's" line, learner produces theirs using any vocabulary learned across the 40 days):

> 1. Greet the other person.
> 2. Introduce yourself and say where you're from.
> 3. Ask how they are / talk briefly about your family or routine.
> 4. Order something (food/drink) or ask for directions.
> 5. Express an opinion or feeling about something ("Mujhe ___ pasand hai" / "Main ___ hoon kyunki...").
> 6. Make a simple plan ("Kal milte hain" / future tense sentence).
> 7. Say goodbye politely.

This mirrors every single "End Goal" item from the original course brief (introduce self, greet, ask/answer questions, talk about family, daily activities, shop/order food, ask directions, express needs, hold a simple conversation) in one final task.
